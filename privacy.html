Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Postiz (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects and uses information when you use our social media scheduling application. Postiz is designed for personal use and small team collaboration to schedule and manage social media content.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication data used to create and access your account.
  • Social Media Data: Content you choose to connect, schedule, or publish through integrated platforms (such as TikTok or other social networks).
  • User Content: Posts, captions, media files, and scheduling information you upload or create.
  • Team Data (if applicable): Workspace information, team members, and collaboration activity within shared accounts.
  • Usage Data: Feature usage, logs, and performance analytics to improve reliability.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and technical identifiers for security and stability.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide scheduling and publishing functionality for social media posts
  • Connect and interact with supported social media APIs
  • Enable team collaboration features
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

3. Social Media Integrations

Postiz may connect to third-party platforms such as TikTok or other social media services. When you authorize these connections, we only access the permissions you explicitly grant. We do not post content without your authorization.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We only share data with:

  • Social media platforms when you choose to publish or schedule content
  • Service providers required to operate the application (e.g., hosting or analytics)
  • Legal authorities if required by law

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

7. Children’s Privacy

Postiz is not intended for individuals under the minimum legal age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting us.
